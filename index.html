01Commotion API Platform

Developer platform for voice AI, B2B SaaS. Sole designer with one engineer, from zero to production in 3 weeks, in light and dark.

A self-serve home for Commotion’s voice and language APIs: get a key, hear and pick a voice, test every service, buy credits and ship. Benchmarked against leading developer platforms, with every flow and edge state designed, and delivered side by side with the engineer.

02Platform X, concierge healthcare

Healthcare, B2B2C. Three products in one: CMO desktop console, CMO mobile app and member app, fully bilingual in English and Arabic.

A platform where a personal Chief Medical Officer follows each premium member from the first health signal to a closed case. It started from an extensive list of use cases written by doctors and care professionals, which we turned into a full service blueprint of scenarios, stages and interaction points before any screen was designed. Every experience was designed in English and Arabic, with mirrored RTL navigation, layout and content. AI runs the infrastructure underneath, reading signals, preparing briefs and coordinating the team, but it never surfaces as a feature: members and doctors simply experience care that is fast, prepared and personal.

03Journey Builder, campaigns and agent workflows

Marketing automation, B2B SaaS. A visual builder for campaign journeys and AI agent workflows.

A drag-and-drop canvas where marketing and operations teams build campaign journeys and agent workflows: triggers, channels, waits, conditions, branches, groups and reusable flows. Grounded in desk research and best practices from leading automation tools, and turned into a practical component system of nodes, connections and interaction states.

04AI Workers, agent configuration and management

Enterprise AI, B2B SaaS. Build, test, deploy and monitor AI Workers. Design Lead, end-to-end platform design.

AI Workers are digital team members that plan, reason and execute real tasks across a company’s tech stack, following its SOPs and guardrails, with humans in the loop when needed. I designed the entire platform to configure and manage them: a multi-agent workflow canvas, agent setup with prompts, tools, knowledge and variables, voice configuration, test simulations that score every scenario before going live, and observability that turns every call into an auditable record.
